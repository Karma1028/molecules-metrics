case 01 · internship
Data Science Intern · Infoleap

From raw exports to a decision view

problem

Stakeholders were reading decisions off raw spreadsheet exports — slow, inconsistent, and easy to misread.

approach

Cleaned and modelled the data in Python, defined the few metrics that actually drove the call, and shipped a repeatable reporting view instead of one-off sheets.

result

A single source of truth that cut the manual prep each cycle and made the numbers defensible.

approach

case 03 · engineering
B.Tech Chemical Engineering

De-bottlenecking a process unit

problem

A unit operation capping throughput — the classic single constraint on the whole line.

approach

Mass + energy balance to locate the true limit, then tested changes against it rather than tweaking the parts that were already fine.

result

Identified the constraint and the change that relieved it — the same instinct I now bring to slow pipelines.
