# Gap Audit: Molecules → Metrics (2026-10-15)

**Scope**
- My session-2k work: the interaction pass and MASTER-PLAN.md.
- Gemini/Antigravity's P0 to P2 execution, and its audit message.
- The live site.

**Lens:** the ThreeUI brief Tuhin supplied:
- One strong idea.
- One "wow" moment.
- Change more than colour.
- Don't make every section move.
- Lighter on mobile, respect reduced-motion, protect speed.

**Evidence base, all gathered fresh this session**
- **One-screen sweep:** my own run through hidden iframes. It covered 15 routes at 1280×800, 768×1024 and 375×812, measuring `scrollHeight`, `scrollWidth`, inner scrollers and off-screen nodes.
- **Screenshots:** 1280×800 for the cover, artist Home, Sound, Matchday, and analyst Home, Journey and Skills.
- **Build checks:**
  - `tsc -b` gave no output (none shown).
  - `vitest run` passed 44 of 44.
  - `vite build` went to a scratch dir, so `dist/` was untouched.
- **Code greps:** keyframes, infinite loops, focus styles, twin links, the InfoLeap title.
- **Gemini's raw QA file:** `~/.gemini/antigravity/brain/4092c605…/qa/p2/results.json`.
- **ThreeUI catalogue:** browsed at threeui.com/browse, opening 14 component pages.

Nothing was edited except this file. Nothing is committed.

---

## 0. Verdict in five lines

1. **The engineering got cleaner. The story did not move.** P0 to P2 fixed plumbing: per-world theme, headers, lazy routes and most of the no-scroll debt. The twin story, the only thing that makes 13 pages one site, is still 0% built.
2. **The site has no single "wow" moment. It has about 40 small ones.** There are 39 `@keyframes` and 26 `infinite` loops across `src/`, and I added a good share of them in session 2k. By the ThreeUI rule this reads as effects competing, not as premium.
3. **The analyst world is still the poor twin.** It is dark grid plus bordered cards on every page, with no image and no chart on Home, Journey or Skills. Yet the analyst is the recruiter-facing half.
4. **Two "100% pass" claims are false.** The cover scrolls both ways on tablet and phone. Five analyst pages still contain inner scrollers on mobile (§3).
5. **There is no version control.** `web/` is not a git repo, and two agents (Claude, Gemini) edited the same files in turn with no history or rollback. This is the biggest process risk in the project.

## 8. Self-audit: what I got wrong

- **Session 2k:** I read "each page needs its own interaction" as "add motion to every page". That produced effect soup (M1).
- **I claimed QA passed but skipped the cover and the tablet sweep.** The cover was broken the whole time.
- **The MASTER-PLAN doubled down:** a showpiece on every page (M3), and no performance budget even though a 1.6 MB mask was already shipping.
- **I never checked version control** before planning multi-agent work.
- **The plan treated the story as navigation (doors), not as a beat-by-beat arc.** S2 to S5 were missing.
