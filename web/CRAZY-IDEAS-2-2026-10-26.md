# More crazy ideas, batch 2 (2026-10-26)

Same rules as batch 1: real data or physics only, nothing invented about you, one wow per page, one screen, opt-in sound. These go further out.

## Chemistry meets football meets photography (your three worlds colliding)

11. **Photo as a titration.** The gallery frames are drops in a burette. Drag the stopcock slowly and frames drip one by one into a beaker whose colour is the running average colour of everything dripped so far (computed from the real pixels). When you stop, the beaker colour is the roll's palette. Chemistry class as a gallery. Effort M.

12. **Barça pressing as a boiling point.** A heat gauge fed by real StatsBomb pressure events from one open-data match. Scrub the match and the liquid in the flask boils where the team pressed hardest. Effort L, open-data licence check first.

13. **Periodic table of the site.** Every page is an element tile with a symbol and atomic number (Gallery = Ga, Sound = So, Notes = No...). The nav is the table; hovering a tile shows its "reactivity" = real visit count this session. Inspiration: peppa's one-object-many-words and stripedev's index-as-interface. Effort S-M.

14. **Molecule built from your route.** Each page you visit adds an atom to a molecule floating in the margin, bonded in visit order, same thread logic as the kantha path. On the analyst side the molecule becomes a bar chart. This is "molecules to metrics" literally. Effort M.

## Playful physics

15. **Gravity switch.** One tiny button flips the page's gravity: every card slowly falls into a pile at the bottom, you can fling them around with the pointer, then they spring back. Never on by default, never breaks one-screen. Effort M.

16. **Horse that follows the thread.** The mascot walks along your visit thread in the margin, stitch by stitch, and rests on the newest one. Real path = your own route. Effort S.

17. **Shadow clock.** A long shadow of the mascot rotates across the page with the real sun position in Goa. At noon it's a puddle under the horse. Pure astronomy. Effort S-M.

## Sound

18. **Spotify years as a vinyl rack.** Eight years of listening become eight records. Pull one out and the sleeve flips (the sleeve view already exists); the groove spacing is the real track count that year. Effort M.

19. **Heartbeat of the repo.** Real GitHub commit timestamps become a soft click track. Press and hold anywhere on the Analyst side to hear a year of commits play in two seconds. Opt-in. Effort S-M.

## Meta and trust

20. **Prove-it mode.** Press `?` and every number on the page gets a small badge linking to its source: `live`, `snapshot 3 Oct`, `static`. The honest-feed horse, taken to its end. Effort M.

21. **View-source poem.** The HTML source starts with a short poem that is also valid markup (comments only, no content changes). People who open view-source find it. Effort S.

22. **Konami goes to the other world.** Up-up-down-down... flips the entire site to the other world with the sky transition running backwards. Effort S, uses code that exists.
