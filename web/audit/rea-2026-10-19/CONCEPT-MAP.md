# Concept map: reference devices moulded to OUR story (Opus, 2026-10-19)

Rule for every row: a device from the teardown is only used once it serves the R2 spine,
**"he watched, then he counted"** (CREATIVE-DIRECTION-R2 §1). Artist world = what he watched and felt.
Analyst world = what he counted. The bridge = the moment watching turned into counting.
The bridge object is the **ball** (R2 build-order #6: the world-switch wow is ball → chart).
Chem-eng drawing language stays out (rejected 2026-10-15). Orange = the artist still in the room.

## What changed in v2 (why the first pointers were not enough)
v1 copied inkwell (ink ring) and the3key (crosshair). They were well tuned (ring t63 221 ms vs inkwell 218),
but they said nothing about Tuhin. v2 keeps the measured physics and changes *what* moves:
the ball is the object he watched; the analyst world measures it, and measures you.
