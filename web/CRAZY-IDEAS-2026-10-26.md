# Crazy ideas (2026-10-26)

You asked for wild ones and the earlier file was mostly practical. These are the wild ones. Every idea runs only on real data (photo EXIF, Spotify history, StatsBomb open data, GitHub, Medium, the visitor's own session) or on physics. No invented facts about you.

Benchmarks used: your 40 reference sites (moonsafari, inkwell, yauch, kashyap, onelastbeat, peppa, teletech, pienso and the rest) plus jameschugh.com and huyml.com. Measured table is in `REFERENCES-AND-IDEAS.md`.

## The wild ten

1. **Exposure time is the hold time (Gallery).** Press and hold a frame to "expose" it. How long you must hold equals that photo's real shutter speed from its EXIF, stretched 1000x, so a 1/30 s night shot develops in a flash and a 2 s long exposure makes you wait. Print appears like a darkroom tray. Inspiration: yauch's waiting-is-content countdown. Effort M.

2. **Lantern cursor (Gallery, dark frames).** On the night frames your cursor becomes a lantern. The photo is black until you sweep light over it. Inspiration: inkwell and moonsafari cursor-as-medium. Effort S-M.

3. **404 penalty shootout (any missing page).** A broken link drops you on a penalty spot. Flick the orange ball with the same flick physics now on the Analyst home. Score and you get sent home; miss and the keeper (the Bankura horse) saves it. Reuses `flick.ts`. Effort S. Idea #23 in the main list, now concrete.

4. **Time machine scrubber (Gallery).** One slider runs 2019 to 2025 using the real capture dates. Frames fade in and out along the timeline and the grain changes with the camera. Dates are real EXIF. Effort M.

5. **Play the site (opt-in sound).** Typing letters plays a pentatonic scale on the radio synth. Typing a page name plays that page's motif and jumps there. Inspiration: onelastbeat's "better with headphones" gate. Off by default. Effort M.

6. **Herd of horses (footer).** Scroll or flick fast and terracotta Bankura horses gallop across the footer, one per page you have visited this session (ties to the kantha thread). Effort M.

7. **Pass network as navigation (Matchday).** Draw a real StatsBomb Barça passing network as a kantha-stitch thread between players. Clicking a pass plays the sequence and reveals the next section. Open data only, no invented stats. Effort L.

8. **Embroider my route (Contact).** The visitor's own thread (already in the margin) turns into a stitched signature on Contact. Download it as a bookmark PNG. Stays on the device. Effort M.

9. **Sun-true light (both worlds).** The site's light angle follows the real computed sun position in Goa, not an API. Golden hour warms the artist world. Pure astronomy, no claims about you. Effort S-M.

10. **Ask the resume in SQL (Analyst).** A command palette (press `/`) accepts `select ... from work` against the real work ledger and answers with the same DAX/lineage panel the cards already use. Inspiration: kashyap's craft-is-the-interface. Effort M.
