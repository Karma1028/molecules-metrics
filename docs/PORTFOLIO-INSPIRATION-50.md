# Portfolio Inspiration: 50 verified sites for Molecules -> Metrics

Fifty personal portfolios (2023 to 2026) with 3D, WebGL or heavily choreographed motion, weighted toward people who show more than one side of their career (composer + designer, engineer + data, photographer + developer, art director + coder).
Every URL below was fetched live on 2026-10-16 and returned a real page (HTTP 200, own content, no parked or unrelated redirect). Houmahani Kane's TLS handshake is flaky from my network (loaded on 2 of 5 tries), so retry if it fails.
How picked: I scraped Awwwards listings (portfolio and Three.js categories, Site of the Day pages) and mined the Muzli 2023, 2024 and 2025 portfolio roundups, then filtered for individuals with a multi-sided story or a distinctive navigation idea, then fetched each site and sniffed its HTML and JS bundles for libraries.

## How to read the data (honest limits)

- **Year / source**: for Awwwards entries the year is the Awwwards award or listing date shown on the linked Awwwards page (SOTD = Site of the Day, DevAward = Developer Award). Where only "listed" is shown there was no SOTD label on the page. For Muzli entries the year is the year of the Muzli roundup that featured the site, not necessarily its launch date (a site can be older). Bruno Simon's year comes from Awwwards' own case study dated 2026-03-11.
- **3D tech** is detected by grepping the page and up to 10 script bundles (Three.js, R3F, GSAP, Lenis, Barba, Pixi, GLSL shader strings, Rapier/Cannon), plus Awwwards tags and descriptions. It is a heuristic: lazy-loaded chunks can hide a library, and "not detected" does not mean absent. "(per Awwwards)" means I am relying on the Awwwards tags or description, not on a bundle match.
- **Block bullets** are my analysis from each site's own metadata/description and the Awwwards write-up, plus the visible text of the page. I did not play-test every interaction in a browser, so "signature interaction" lines say what the description claims; open each link to confirm feel.
- Dropped for failing verification from my environment: gionatannese.com and haoqi.design (connection reset on every attempt), gsdev-creative.com (no connection), omid.bio (redirects to a parked domain), dougl.co (404), constancesouville.com (526), max-milkin.com.ua (now a gambling-news site).

Molecules -> Metrics page names used below: Home, Cross-world transition, Gallery, Sound/Radio, Matchday, Dashboard, Journey, Resume, Work.

## 2. Per-site notes

Format: Special / Sides / Signature / Steal (page) / Risk.

### 1. Michael Gatt
- Special: a "universe" of film, TV, game and music composing, with a click-anywhere sound gate before the work.
- Sides: composer identity split across media types (film/TV vs games) inside one world instead of tabs.
- Signature: sound-on or enter-without-sound choice at the door, then shader-backed 3D scenes (Three.js + GLSL detected).
- Steal: Sound/Radio and Home. The "sound on / enter silent" gate fits the Hostel Radio page and a first-visit consent for audio.
- Risk: autoplay audio needs a user gesture (they solve it with the gate); heavy shader bundles on mobile.

### 2. Jacob How
- Special: music producer, animator and UX designer in one page, with a live Miami clock and links to Bandcamp, Spotify and Apple Music.
- Sides: "Music" is a first-class section next to design, not a footnote.
- Signature: music-led animation and visual systems (per meta); local-time detail makes it feel inhabited.
- Steal: Sound/Radio plus Home. A live IST clock and "now playing" strip on the radio page; music links as a peer of work.
- Risk: 3D library not detected, so effects may be mostly CSS/canvas; check it before borrowing motion.
